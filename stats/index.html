About these numbers

Counted: views per page, hour and weekday, referrer domains, countries and US states, device types, browser families and screen sizes, plus bucketed visit length, scroll depth, outbound domains and page-to-page moves. Visit numbers are estimates.

Counts started Nov 1, 2026, with the site’s own counter. Device, browser, entry, exit and time-on-page counts begin then; the league’s earlier views have page and place only.

Countries and states are shown as shares: GoatCounter works places out a little differently from page views, so its place counts run slightly above the totals.
