The ground rules for using this site and its apps, in plain English. Short version: it's a hobby site, everything is free and offered as-is, your saved data is yours, and please be kind to it.

What this site is

Off the Wall is my personal site: a portfolio of hobby apps I build for fun. It isn't a business. Nothing is sold here, no payments are taken, and the apps are provided as-is.

Use at your own risk

The apps and tools are free, with no guarantee that they'll work, be accurate, or stay available. Don't rely on them for anything important: check flight and hotel details with the airline or hotel, financial figures with your bank or card issuer, and snow and surf conditions with the official forecasts and reports before you go.

Accounts and your data

Some apps let you sign in so your own data is saved. What you save is yours, and you can delete it in the app: Contrail and What's in My Wallet? have a delete button in Settings, Budget Ledger in Manage → Your data, and Snow & Surf Report in Notifications. The privacy page explains what's kept and how to have the rest removed.

The demos show made-up sample data (Alex & Jordan are fictional). If I approve you for an app, that access is for you personally, so please don't share your account. I can remove access if an account is misused.

Acceptable use

Please don't try to break the site, get around its sign-in, scrape it heavily, or overload it or the services behind it. Don't use the apps for anything unlawful.
