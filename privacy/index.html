Privacy

What this site and its apps collect, and what they don't. Short version: page views are counted in aggregate, there are no cookies, and if you sign in to Budget Ledger, your budget is private to your account.

Visit counts

Page views are counted with GoatCounter, in aggregate only. It keeps no IP addresses and sets no cookies. The site sends the page's path (never the rest of the address), the domain of the site that linked you here, your device type and browser family, and how far down the page you scrolled. GoatCounter uses your IP address only to look up your country and US state, then discards it.

A small Cloudflare Worker reads those totals to draw the site's statistics page: views by page, referring domain, country and US state, and device and browser family. It sees only totals, never individual visits.

Snow & Surf Report

Without signing in, your alert choices, pinned places and Rider Profile are saved only on your own device, in your browser. If you use “Use my location”, your location is rounded to about 7 miles before it's saved.

If you sign in, the same things are also saved to your account (see Accounts), along with any other email address you choose for alerts and each device you turn on morning notifications for, so the morning job knows what to send. Alert emails are sent from a Gmail account. The Notifications tab's “Delete my saved data” button removes it from your device and your account.

Accounts

Budget Ledger and Snow & Surf Report let you sign in with Google, so your budget or your alert setup is saved to your own account. Contrail and What's in My Wallet? do too, for people I've approved: their trips, stays, cards and wallet save to their account. If you ask for access, your request is kept with your sign-in name and email, and whether I approved or declined it; I review requests on a private admin page that only I can open. Approved members also have a record of which apps they can use and whether the AI and Gmail features are switched on for them. One sign-in covers every app. It goes through Supabase, which keeps your name, email address and the link to your Google profile picture. Signing in shares nothing else: not your Gmail, contacts or Drive. (Contrail's Gmail import is a separate step you choose; see below.)

What you save is stored by Supabase on servers in the US (Oregon). The database's security rules let only you read or change it; other visitors and other signed-in people can't. A nightly copy of the database is kept in a private GitHub repository, so it can be restored if something goes wrong.

Your sign-in is remembered in your browser's own storage (not a cookie) until you sign out. Signing out of Budget Ledger also removes the copy of your budget kept on that device. The other apps don't use accounts.

Contrail: importing from Gmail

Members I've switched Gmail import on for can connect their Gmail in Contrail's Settings. Contrail then asks Google for read-only access to your mail. That access works in your browser only: it lasts about an hour, is kept in that browser tab, and no server ever has it.

Contrail searches your mail for booking emails from airlines, hotels, rental sites and travel sites (confirmations, itineraries, receipts, changes and cancellations) and reads each one in your browser. When an email has the booking details built in, as many airline and hotel emails do, Contrail uses those directly. Otherwise it sends the email's sender, subject, date and text (up to about 12,000 characters, without images or attachments) to a small Cloudflare Worker of mine, which checks you're a member with Gmail import on and passes the text to Anthropic's Claude to pick out the flight or stay. The answer comes back to your browser. Anthropic's handling of that text is covered by its privacy policy.

Nothing keeps the emails themselves. What's saved to your account is only what was found (flights, the hotel, dates and confirmation numbers) and, for each email checked, its Gmail message ID and whether it was added, updated or skipped, so it isn't read twice. The sync log in Settings shows the same, with the sender's domain for skipped emails. The Worker counts how many emails you've had read today, to keep within a daily limit, and its log keeps only a short fingerprint of each email's text, the outcome and its size, never the text.

Disconnect in Settings removes Contrail's access to your Gmail. You can also remove it any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Trips and stays added from email are deleted along with the rest of your Contrail data (see below).

Cookies

None. Some pages remember small settings, like light or dark mode, in your browser's own storage on your device; those stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. If you sign in, your sign-in is kept there too (see Accounts).

Deleting your data

On your device: clear it in the app (Snow & Surf has a button for it) or clear this site's data in your browser.

In Snow & Surf: Notifications → “Delete my saved data” removes your setup from your device and your account. In Budget Ledger: Manage → Your data → “Delete my saved budget” removes it from the server straight away. In Contrail: Settings → “Delete my saved Contrail data”, and in What's in My Wallet?: Settings → “Delete my saved wallet”, do the same for your trips, stays and cards. Copies in older nightly backups remain until those are cleared, so to have them removed too, or to delete your account itself, email me using the address on the Contact page and I'll delete it.
