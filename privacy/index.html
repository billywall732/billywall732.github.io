Privacy

What this site and its apps collect, and what they don't. Short version: page views are counted in aggregate, there are no cookies, and if you sign in to Budget Ledger, your budget is private to your account.

Visit counts

Page views are counted with GoatCounter, in aggregate only. It keeps no IP addresses and sets no cookies. The site sends the page's path (never the rest of the address), the domain of the site that linked you here, your device type and browser family, and how far down the page you scrolled. GoatCounter uses your IP address only to look up your country and US state, then discards it.

A small Cloudflare Worker reads those totals to draw the site's statistics page: views by page, referring domain, country and US state, and device and browser family. It sees only totals, never individual visits.

Snow & Surf Report

Without signing in, your alert choices, pinned places and Rider Profile are saved only on your own device, in your browser. If you use “Use my location”, your location is rounded to about 7 miles before it's saved.

If you sign in, the same things are also saved to your account (see Accounts), along with any other email address you choose for alerts and each device you turn on morning notifications for, so the morning job knows what to send. Alert emails are sent from a Gmail account. The Notifications tab's “Delete my saved data” button removes it from your device and your account.

Accounts

Budget Ledger and Snow & Surf Report let you sign in with Google, so your budget or your alert setup is saved to your own account. One sign-in covers both. It goes through Supabase, which keeps your name, email address and the link to your Google profile picture. Google shares nothing else: not your Gmail, contacts or Drive.

What you save is stored by Supabase on servers in the US (Oregon). The database's security rules let only you read or change it; other visitors and other signed-in people can't. A nightly copy of the database is kept in a private GitHub repository, so it can be restored if something goes wrong.

Your sign-in is remembered in your browser's own storage (not a cookie) until you sign out. Signing out of Budget Ledger also removes the copy of your budget kept on that device. The other apps don't use accounts.

Cookies

None. Some pages remember small settings, like light or dark mode, in your browser's own storage on your device; those stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. If you sign in, your sign-in is kept there too (see Accounts).

Deleting your data

On your device: clear it in the app (Snow & Surf has a button for it) or clear this site's data in your browser.

In Snow & Surf: Notifications → “Delete my saved data” removes your setup from your device and your account. In Budget Ledger: Manage → Your data → “Delete my saved budget” removes it from the server straight away. Copies in older nightly backups remain until those are cleared, so to have them removed too, or to delete your account itself, email me using the address on the Contact page and I'll delete it.
