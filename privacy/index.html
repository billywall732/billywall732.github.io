Privacy

What this site and its apps collect, and what they don't. Short version: page views are counted in aggregate, nothing identifies you, and there are no cookies.

Visit counts

Page views are counted with GoatCounter, in aggregate only. It keeps no IP addresses and sets no cookies. The site sends the page's path (never the rest of the address), the domain of the site that linked you here, your device type and browser family, and how far down the page you scrolled. GoatCounter uses your IP address only to look up your country and US state, then discards it.

A small Cloudflare Worker reads those totals to draw the site's statistics page: views by page, referring domain, country and US state, and device and browser family. It sees only totals, never individual visits.

Snow & Surf Report

Your alert choices, pinned places and Rider Profile are saved only on your own device, in your browser. If you use “Use my location”, your location is rounded to about 7 miles before it's saved, and it's saved only on your device. The Notifications tab has a “Delete my saved data” button that removes all of it.

Accounts

Google sign-in is turned off for now, so no account data is collected. The apps keep what you enter on your own device. If sign-in comes back, this page will say what it stores before it does.

Cookies

None. Some pages remember small settings, like light or dark mode, in your browser's own storage on your device. That stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Everything an app saved is on your device: clear it in the app (Snow & Surf has a button for it) or clear this site's data in your browser. For anything else, email me using the address on the Contact page and I'll delete it.
